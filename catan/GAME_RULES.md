# Catan Game Rules

## 1. Setup
- The board is set up with 19 terrain hexes surrounded by 6 frame pieces with ports.
- Number tokens are placed on the hexes.
- Each player chooses a color and receives 5 settlements, 4 cities, and 15 roads.
- Players take turns placing two settlements and two roads on the board.

## 2. Turn Overview
Each player's turn consists of the following phases:
1.  **Roll Dice**: Roll two dice to determine resource production.
2.  **Trade**: Trade resources with other players or with the bank/ports.
3.  **Build**: Build roads, settlements, cities, or buy development cards.

## 3. Resource Production
- Each hex produces a specific resource:
    - Forest -> Lumber
    - Hills -> Brick
    - Pasture -> Wool
    - Fields -> Grain
    - Mountains -> Ore
    - Desert -> Nothing
- If the dice roll matches the number on a hex, all players with a settlement/city on that hex receive the corresponding resource.

## 4. Trading
- **Domestic Trade**: Players can trade resources with each other.
- **Maritime Trade**: Players can trade 4:1 with the bank, or 3:1/2:1 if they have a settlement on a port.

## 5. Building Costs
- **Road**: 1 Brick + 1 Lumber
- **Settlement**: 1 Brick + 1 Lumber + 1 Wool + 1 Grain
- **City**: 2 Grain + 3 Ore
- **Development Card**: 1 Wool + 1 Grain + 1 Ore

## 6. The Robber
- If a 7 is rolled:
    - No resources are produced.
    - Players with more than 7 cards must discard half (rounded down).
    - The active player moves the robber to a new hex and steals a random card from a player on that hex.

## 7. Winning
The first player to reach 10 Victory Points (VP) wins.
- **Settlement**: 1 VP
- **City**: 2 VP
- **Longest Road**: 2 VP
- **Largest Army**: 2 VP
- **Victory Point Cards**: 1 VP each
